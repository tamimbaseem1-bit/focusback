FocusBack

SAIF 2026 · proof of concept

A private nudge back to the task, that learns what “on task” looks like for each student.

Looking down can mean reading or drifting off. FocusBack checks the current task and the student’s own pattern before it alerts, and learns from “I was focused”.

Student page

Opens on the device in front of the student: iPad or laptop with a camera.

  • Camera and calibration for Reading, Writing or Listening
  • A soft flash and sound when attention drifts
  • “I’m back” and “I was focused”

Privacy

  • The camera is analysed on the student’s device.
  • Video is never saved, and never sent to the teacher, the parent or any server.
  • Only numbers and events travel between the pages.
  • Students use a code like P01 instead of a name.
  • Losing sight of the face is logged as “Tracking lost”, never as off-task.

What we measure

  • False alert rate: the main result. It should fall in Round 2.
  • Real off-task detection: should stay high after learning.
  • Attention recovery time: seconds from alert to return.
  • Demo Mode rounds (no camera) never count in the results.
  • Future work: move the alert to a smart wristband or smartwatch.
